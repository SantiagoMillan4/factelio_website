Factelio mobile app

Privacy policy

Updated September 28, 2026

Factelio is an invoicing app that works primarily on your device. This page explains how information is handled in the mobile app.

Information stored on your device

Your clients, invoices, business profile, preferences, and Pro purchase status are stored locally. Factelio does not require an account and has no built-in analytics or advertising service.

Sharing and purchases

When you choose to share a PDF or export a backup, the file goes to the destination you select. That destination has its own privacy practices. Pro purchases and restores go through your device’s app store; Factelio does not receive your payment details.

Your choices

You can edit or delete clients and invoices in the app. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data according to your device and backup settings. Files you exported remain wherever you saved them.

Questions

Use “Send feedback” in the app’s settings for privacy questions.
